Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 20, 2026

1. Introduction

LockLink is a secure digital delivery platform designed for freelancers, creators, and digital service providers. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect information when visitors use our website or express interest in our services.

2. Information We May Collect

We may collect basic information that you voluntarily provide, such as your name, email address, contact details, or messages submitted through our website or early access forms.

When the LockLink platform launches, we may also collect information related to account creation, digital delivery links, transaction records, and customer support requests.

3. How We Use Information

We use collected information to respond to inquiries, provide product updates, improve our services, prevent abuse, maintain platform security, and prepare future access to LockLink.

4. Payments

LockLink plans to use trusted third-party payment providers to process payments securely. We do not intend to store full card numbers or sensitive payment credentials on our own servers.

5. Cookies and Analytics

We may use basic cookies or analytics tools to understand website usage, improve performance, and measure interest in our services. Visitors may disable cookies through their browser settings.

6. Data Sharing

We do not sell personal information. We may share limited information with trusted service providers only when necessary to operate the website, process payments, provide support, prevent fraud, or comply with legal obligations.

7. Data Security

We aim to protect user information through reasonable technical and organizational safeguards. However, no internet-based service can guarantee absolute security.

8. Data Retention

We retain personal information only for as long as reasonably necessary to provide services, respond to inquiries, comply with legal obligations, and maintain business records.

9. Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, or restrict the use of your personal information. You may contact us to request assistance regarding your information.
