LockLink Privacy

Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how LockLink collects, uses, stores, shares, and protects information when you visit our website, join early access, contact us, or use our private digital delivery infrastructure.

Last updated: May 20, 2026

Privacy summary

LockLink does not sell personal information. We collect only the information needed to operate the website, respond to inquiries, prepare early access, provide private digital delivery features, maintain security, prevent abuse, and comply with legal or payment-provider requirements.

1. Introduction

LockLink is private digital delivery infrastructure for pay-to-unlock delivery links. Sellers may use LockLink to create private delivery pages for already-agreed digital work, and buyers may use LockLink to review protected previews and unlock digital files after payment confirmation.

This Privacy Policy applies to information collected through our website, early access forms, contact forms, abuse reports, support communications, private delivery pages, payment-related workflows, file delivery features, and any related LockLink services.

“LockLink,” “we,” “us,” and “our” refer to the LockLink service. “You” and “your” refer to visitors, sellers, buyers, early access users, and anyone interacting with LockLink.

2. Information we collect

The information we collect depends on how you use LockLink and which features are available at the time.

Information you provide directly

  • name, email address, and contact details;
  • messages sent through contact, support, abuse, or business inquiry forms;
  • early access sign-up information;
  • seller profile or business information if accounts are enabled;
  • buyer contact information where needed for delivery or support;
  • deal titles, descriptions, prices, file information, and delivery settings;
  • support requests, abuse reports, takedown requests, and related evidence.

Information related to digital delivery

  • uploaded files and file metadata, such as file name, type, size, checksum, timestamps, and storage references;
  • protected previews, thumbnails, screenshots, watermarked previews, or limited metadata created for review before payment;
  • private deal links, deal IDs, access status, expiry windows, download limits, and delivery records;
  • payment-confirmation status and unlock records;
  • download attempts, access timestamps, device/browser signals, and security logs.

Information collected automatically

  • IP address, browser type, device type, operating system, language settings, and approximate location derived from technical signals;
  • pages viewed, links clicked, referring pages, session activity, and website performance data;
  • cookies, local storage, and similar technologies used for preferences, analytics, security, and functionality;
  • error logs, diagnostic logs, fraud-prevention signals, and abuse-prevention data.

Information from third parties

We may receive limited information from third-party service providers, such as payment providers, hosting providers, analytics providers, fraud-prevention tools, email providers, storage providers, or security vendors. This may include payment status, transaction identifiers, risk signals, account verification status, support metadata, or technical delivery events.

3. How we use information

We use information to operate, secure, improve, and support LockLink.

  • to provide the website and private delivery infrastructure;
  • to process early access requests and product updates;
  • to create, display, secure, and manage private delivery links;
  • to store files privately and generate protected previews where applicable;
  • to confirm payment status and unlock digital file access after successful payment confirmation;
  • to limit download access, enforce expiry windows, and prevent unauthorized access;
  • to respond to contact, support, business, and abuse inquiries;
  • to detect, prevent, and investigate fraud, malware, rights violations, prohibited content, and misuse;
  • to maintain service security, troubleshoot errors, and improve performance;
  • to comply with legal, regulatory, tax, accounting, payment-provider, and enforcement obligations;
  • to protect the rights, safety, property, and integrity of LockLink, users, service providers, and the public.

4. Payment information

LockLink may use third-party payment providers to process payments, confirm transactions, manage platform fees, support seller payouts, and handle payment-related compliance. LockLink does not intend to store full payment card numbers or sensitive card authentication data on its own servers.

Payment providers may collect and process payment details, identity information, billing information, transaction data, risk signals, tax information, payout information, and compliance documentation according to their own terms and privacy policies.

We may receive limited payment-related information, such as transaction identifiers, payment status, confirmation events, refund status, chargeback status, currency, amount, provider fees, account status, or risk-review results, when needed to operate LockLink.

5. Files, previews, and delivery data

If file upload and delivery features are enabled, sellers may upload digital files to LockLink. We may store uploaded files privately and process them to create protected previews, thumbnails, file metadata, access controls, and secure download workflows.

We do not use uploaded files to create a public marketplace listing unless the seller explicitly uses a feature that publishes information publicly. LockLink is designed around private delivery links for already-agreed digital work.

Files, previews, and delivery metadata may be reviewed or restricted if needed for abuse prevention, security, legal compliance, support, takedown requests, payment-provider requirements, or enforcement of our Terms.

6. Cookies, local storage, and analytics

LockLink may use cookies, local storage, and similar technologies to remember preferences, measure website performance, understand product interest, improve user experience, protect against abuse, and support security.

For example, the website may store your language preference locally in your browser. Analytics tools, if enabled, may collect usage information such as page views, device type, approximate location, and referral source.

You can control cookies through your browser settings. Some features may not work correctly if cookies or local storage are disabled.

7. How we share information

We do not sell personal information. We may share information only where needed to operate, secure, support, or legally protect LockLink.

  • Service providers: hosting, storage, email, analytics, security, database, infrastructure, customer support, and operational providers;
  • Payment providers: payment processors, payout providers, fraud tools, banking partners, and compliance providers;
  • Other users: limited deal information may be shown between sellers and buyers through private delivery pages;
  • Legal and safety: regulators, courts, law enforcement, rights holders, payment providers, hosting providers, or security partners where required or appropriate;
  • Business transfers: if LockLink is involved in a merger, acquisition, financing, restructuring, or sale of assets, information may be transferred as part of that process.

8. International processing

LockLink and its service providers may process information in countries other than your country of residence. Data protection laws may differ depending on where information is processed. Where required, we will use reasonable safeguards for international transfers.

9. Data retention

We keep information only as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described in this Privacy Policy, including providing the service, maintaining security, complying with legal obligations, resolving disputes, enforcing our Terms, meeting payment-provider requirements, and keeping business records.

Files, private links, previews, delivery records, and download access may expire or be deleted according to deal settings, retention rules, account status, abuse reports, technical requirements, or legal obligations.

Early access emails and contact messages may be kept until they are no longer needed for product updates, support, business records, or compliance.

10. Data security

We use reasonable technical and organizational safeguards designed to protect information against unauthorized access, loss, misuse, alteration, and disclosure. These safeguards may include private storage, access controls, limited download windows, encryption where appropriate, security monitoring, and restricted operational access.

No internet-based service can guarantee absolute security. You should keep your own backups, protect your accounts, use secure devices, and avoid sharing private links with unauthorized people.

11. Your choices and rights

Depending on your location, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, restrict, object to, or request a copy of your personal information. You may also have the right to withdraw consent where processing is based on consent.

To make a privacy request, contact us using the email below. We may need to verify your identity before processing certain requests. Some information may need to be retained for legal, security, fraud-prevention, payment, tax, or operational reasons.

12. Marketing and product updates

If you sign up for early access or product updates, we may use your email to send launch news, product updates, feature announcements, and relevant business communications. You may request removal from these communications at any time by contacting us.

13. Children’s privacy

LockLink is not intended for children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under the age required by applicable law. If you believe a child provided personal information to LockLink, contact us so we can review and take appropriate action.

14. Prohibited or illegal content reports

If LockLink receives a report involving illegal content, rights violations, malware, abuse, fraud, or prohibited activity, we may preserve, review, restrict, remove, or disclose relevant information where needed to investigate the report, enforce our Terms, protect users, prevent harm, or comply with applicable law.

15. Third-party links and services

LockLink may link to or integrate with third-party websites, payment providers, hosting providers, analytics tools, email tools, or other services. Their privacy practices are governed by their own policies. We are not responsible for the privacy practices of third-party services.

16. Pre-launch and feature changes

LockLink may be in early access, testing, or pre-launch mode. Some features described in this Privacy Policy may be planned, limited, unavailable, or subject to change before public launch. As features evolve, the types of information we collect and the way we process information may also change.

17. Changes to this Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The updated version will be posted on this page with a revised “Last updated” date. Continued use of LockLink after changes means you accept the updated Privacy Policy.

18. Contact

For privacy questions, data requests, support, or abuse-related privacy concerns, contact LockLink at: support@locklink.net.

Plain-language summary

LockLink collects information needed to run private pay-to-unlock digital delivery links, keep files protected, confirm payment status, prevent abuse, and support users. We do not sell personal information. Payment providers may process payment data under their own policies. Uploaded files and private deal data are handled as private delivery infrastructure, not public marketplace listings.
